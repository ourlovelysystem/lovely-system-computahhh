## What it is

A provisional toy for dividing communication into sequential slices and assigning each slice two interpretive coordinates:

- **Signal strength**
- **Information density**

The intended fibril is a trajectory through stacked coordinate planes. A flat quadrant chart is only a diagnostic projection.

## What it is not

The Fibrillator is not:

- validated measurement science;
- a truth detector;
- a moral classifier;
- a judge of speakers;
- a basis for consequential decisions.

Information does not mean truth.

Signal does not mean virtue.

Moral judgment remains outside the toy.

## Permitted play

- Compare sequential motion.
- Produce tables and experimental visualizations.
- Invite hostile analysis.
- Rescore.
- Surface disagreement.
- Break the model.
