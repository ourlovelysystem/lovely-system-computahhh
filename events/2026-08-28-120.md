# Event 120 — Data Leakage Accepted as Transparency Frontier Risk

**Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Projects:** Computahhh, Q&A logging  
**Participants:** Will Daly and ChatGPT/Codex  
**Related record:** [Event 119](./2026-08-28-119.md)  
**Related artifacts:** `artifacts/event-119-01.png` through `artifacts/event-119-04.png`

## Event

After Event 119 was published, Will supplied four screen captures of his request to ChatGPT/Codex and the resulting evaluation. At Will's direction, ChatGPT/Codex uploaded the screen captures to the public Computahhh repository as artifacts without describing them.

Will then said:

> I leaked data in the screen captures. Evaluate risk.

ChatGPT/Codex evaluated the visible disclosure as low to moderate risk. The screen captures exposed contextual and identifying information, including a ChatGPT conversation URL and conversation identifier, the display name `Will D`, Plus subscription status, sidebar conversation titles, browser tabs and interface details, timestamps, and visible conversation text. ChatGPT/Codex did not identify a visible password, API key, AWS credential, access token, email address, payment record, recovery secret, or public ChatGPT share link.

The assessment classified account-compromise risk as low, credential-exposure risk as very low, and personal or contextual privacy risk as moderate. It also noted that later deletion of the files would not, by itself, remove them from Git history.

## Will's directive and judgment

Will said:

> I recognize tension between the risks of transparency and the risks of secrecy in the general sense. Secrecy cast as privacy is a farce. Leave the screen captures as they are. Surface an event log entry describing my error. Present my directives herein FAITHFULLY. Include my statement as follows.

Will directed that the screen captures remain as published. This event does not remove, replace, revise, obscure, or otherwise alter them.

## Will Daly's statement

> I, Will Daly, do assume responsibility for my error. I classify my error as data leakage. I classify my posture as accepted transparency frontier risk.

## Record status

The data leakage and Will's decision to leave the disclosed material visible are both part of the durable public record.

This entry records Will's classification. It does not relabel the error as harmless, accidental transparency, informed consent before disclosure, or a privacy-policy dispute. Will classified the error as **data leakage** and his posture after evaluating the exposure as **accepted transparency frontier risk**.
