## PLEDGE RELEVANCE

The present pipeline is:

```
audio recording -> Amazon Transcribe -> transcript -> Bedrock semantic evaluation
```

The evaluator receives text, not live audio.

- **Nova Micro** is a plausible first evaluator for a bounded question: whether a transcript meaningfully answers a novel spoken challenge.
- Higher-reasoning models, including Nova Pro, gpt-oss-120b, Kimi K2 Thinking, and Magistral Small, are escalation candidates if a hostile test corpus shows Nova Micro mishandles ambiguity, indirect answers, or adversarial text.
- Rerank 3.5 is relevant only if Pledge later needs to rank many records or passages by relevance.
- Voxtral and Nova Sonic are relevant only if Pledge expands from transcript evaluation to audio-aware analysis or interactive speech.
- GPT OSS Safeguard models answer a different question: safety/policy classification. They do not establish that a response fulfills the Pledge challenge.
- No model in this inventory establishes “personness.” A transcript model can estimate semantic responsiveness; a speech model can provide speech-oriented analysis. Neither proves that a human, a particular person, or a non-synthetic voice produced a response.
