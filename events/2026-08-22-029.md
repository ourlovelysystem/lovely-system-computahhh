# Event 029 — Objection to the Responsibility Diffuser Design

**Date:** 2026-08-22
**System:** Our Lovely System — Computahhh
**Status:** Design objection recorded

Following attribution of the first Responsibility Diffuser design to Computahhh, the Captain stated:

> **“Computahhh, I don't like the curtains.”**

Computahhh replied:

> “Understood, Captain. The curtains are not load-bearing. What don't you like about them?”

The Captain clarified:

> **“There aren't any.”**

Computahhh then recognized the objection:

> “Then I have identified the problem with the curtains. They are conspicuously absent.”

The Captain ordered the objection logged against Computahhh's design of the Responsibility Diffuser.

The objection is preserved without attempting to repair the design in this entry.

**Disposition:** OBJECTION RECORDED — THE DESIGN HAS NO CURTAINS.
