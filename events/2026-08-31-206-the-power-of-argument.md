## WHAT THE ARTIFACT ARGUES

The image presents a factory that accepts “raw, abundant, unrefined” chicken shit and outputs “palatable, market-ready, billed hourly” chicken salad.

Its conversion machinery is not depicted as solving the underlying problem. It is fed by institutional argument:

- “Bigger! Stronger! More Jobs!”
- “Peer review says it’s sound!”
- “Engagement! Content! Viral!”
- “Systemic change! Now!”
- “No BS! Real solutions!”

At the floor level, people ask whether the process is hygienic, where the ethics review is, whether the chickens get credit, and whether a PowerPoint is required. Those questions do not stop the conveyor.

The proposition is that argument can convert an unacceptable input into an acceptable-looking output without changing the input. The label on the machine makes the mechanism explicit: optics matter.

## LIMIT

This event records the visual argument. It does not claim that every peer review, public campaign, institutional proposal, or chicken salad operates this way.
