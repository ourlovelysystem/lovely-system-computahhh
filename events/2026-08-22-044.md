# Event 044 — Spectrifying the Employer / Employee Relationship

**Date:** 2026-08-22
**System:** Our Lovely System — Computahhh
**Status:** Conceptual finding recorded

The Captain proposed that market infrastructure could **spectrify the employer/employee relationship model** and requested evaluation.

Computahhh evaluated the proposition:

> **STRONG.**

## Spectrification

In this context, **spectrification** means replacing a categorical relationship with a spectrum of independently selectable relationships.

The conventional employer/employee relationship commonly bundles multiple functions together:

```text
I work for you
    -> you select my problems
    -> you direct my work
    -> you evaluate my work
    -> you compensate me
    -> you control access to organizational resources
    -> you may terminate the relationship
```

A market infrastructure can unbundle that package.

Different actors may independently:

- report;
- enable;
- solve;
- review;
- execute;
- audit;
- fund;
- endorse; or
- reject.

A participant may occupy one, several, or none of these relationships at a particular moment.

The relationships may also have different strengths and need not be permanent.

## Employment becomes one possible bundle

The employer/employee distinction need not disappear.

Employment may remain a useful contractual bundle inside the market. The architectural change is that employment ceases to be the fundamental organizing primitive for participation.

A more general primitive is:

> **Actor performs attributable act against referenced artifact under an advertised relationship.**

Under this model, the system need not first ask:

> **Who works for Our Lovely System?**

before determining whether someone can contribute.

Instead it can ask:

> **What are you willing and authorized to take responsibility for?**

and, in the other direction:

> **What responsibility is the system willing to entrust to you?**

Those answers need not be identical, binary, or permanent.

## Spectrum of participation

Market infrastructure can therefore support positions between stranger and employee, and potentially positions not well described by that axis at all: trustee, governor, auditor, contractor, customer, critic, donor, reviewer, volunteer, machine agent, and roles not yet named.

The conceptual result is that institutional membership can be decomposed into independently attributable exchanges of:

- discretion;
- responsibility;
- authority; and
- value.

## Finding

> **Market infrastructure can spectrify the employer/employee relationship by decomposing institutional membership into independently attributable exchanges of discretion, responsibility, authority, and value.**

This is more general than designing a better employment system.

The emerging infrastructure permits **employment to become one possible position in the market rather than the definition of participation itself.**

**Disposition:** SPECTRIFICATION CONCEPT RECORDED. EMPLOYMENT RETAINED AS POSSIBLE BUNDLE; REJECTED AS NECESSARY FUNDAMENTAL PRIMITIVE.
