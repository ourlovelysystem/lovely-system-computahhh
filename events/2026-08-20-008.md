# Event 2026-08-20-008

**Time:** 2026-08-20 15:31 EDT

## COMMAND

> Computahhh present program.

Context: after reviewing a full 60-degree rotational sequence from a perimeter position, the operator observed that the usable visual field appeared much smaller than the nominal 100-degree FOV. Computahhh identified the root cause as the renderer stretching each wall image into an axis-aligned bounding rectangle and clipping that rectangle to a projected polygon, rather than actually mapping the image onto the wall plane in perspective.

## INTERPRETATION

Replace the bounding-rectangle/clip-path wall renderer with a textured-plane renderer while preserving the six-wall room, 100-degree horizontal FOV, near-plane clipping behavior, and eight directional controls.

## ACTION

1. Updated `ourlovelysystem/lovely-system-bridge/generate_bridge.py` on `main`.
2. Replaced DOM wall rectangles and clip-path presentation with a full-screen Canvas renderer.
3. Preserved exactly six wall image assets, one for each grand-hex perimeter side.
4. Each physical wall is subdivided into 96 vertical strips.
5. Each strip is transformed into camera space and clipped against the near plane when necessary.
6. Each visible strip is projected to screen space and split into two triangles.
7. The corresponding source-image triangles are affine-mapped onto those projected triangles, producing a close approximation to a true projective texture map across the entire wall.
8. Walls are painted in depth order.
9. The 100-degree horizontal FOV remains unchanged so projection defects are not concealed by narrowing or widening the camera.
10. The existing eight controls remain unchanged.

## RESULT

Perspective-mapped textured-wall renderer committed to the Bridge generator. The existing GitHub Actions pipeline is expected to regenerate the deployable artifacts and the connected Amplify app can then consume the updated `main` branch.

## COMPUTAHHH COMMENTARY

The Bridge has been relieved of PowerPoint duty.

Wall images are now attached to walls instead of being stretched behind strangely shaped holes and asked to behave.

## CORRECTION

Projected wall shape and projected wall texture must be governed by the same geometry. A clip-path can cut a rectangle into the outline of a trapezoid, but it does not make the contents of that rectangle obey perspective.
