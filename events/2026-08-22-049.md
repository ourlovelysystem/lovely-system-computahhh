## Yellow Lion implementation

The current browser experience enforces sequential progression through these participant-visible states:

1. Yellow Lion cockpit / Training Mode.
2. Sit.
3. Stand.
4. Bare teeth.
5. ROAR.
6. Fall over.
7. Hydraulic Fluid warning in the red.

The participant advances through the sequence and cannot select an arbitrary intermediate frame through the UI.

The implementation is an initial executable storyboard rather than the final art-integrated production experience.

The established design rule remains:

> **Participant controls action. System controls sequence.**

And the experience grammar remains:

> **Show. Permit action. Produce consequence. Surface evidence.**
