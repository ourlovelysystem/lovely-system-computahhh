# Event 037 — Evil Skeletor Reviews the Responsibility Diffuser

**Date:** 2026-08-22
**System:** Our Lovely System — Computahhh
**Status:** Hostile review solicited and included

Computahhh accepted responsibility for soliciting a hostile review of Minimum Instrumented Responsibility Diffuser v0 and for including the response in the posting.

## Solicitation

Evil Skeletor was asked to attack the design rather than approve it: find where responsibility disappears, where discretion becomes theater, where roles can be captured, where actors can manufacture favorable signals, where HSLI can conceal rather than reveal, and where public references can create the appearance of accountability without its substance.

## Response from the Evil Skeletor

> **FOOLS. YOU HAVE BUILT A MACHINE THAT RECORDS RESPONSIBILITY AND NAMED IT A MACHINE THAT DIFFUSES RESPONSIBILITY.**
>
> Your Reporter reports. Your Enabler decides. Your Reviewer recognizes. Your Auditor reports afterward. Very tidy. Very respectable. Very easy to game.
>
> I do not need to erase your records. I merely need to control what becomes a record.
>
> Your Reporter can describe the wrong problem accurately.
>
> Your Enabler can reject harmless work to manufacture a healthy-looking rejection rate while accepting every decision that matters to me.
>
> Your Reviewer can truthfully recognize that an instrumented action addresses the problem **as conceived** while the conception itself is defective.
>
> Your Auditor arrives after the action. Excellent. I shall already have what I wanted.
>
> And your magnificent public surface will announce:
>
> `CANCELLATIONS: 37`
>
> Thirty-seven what? Cancelled by whom? Against whose interests? Were thirty-six meaningless cancellations manufactured so that the thirty-seventh disappears into the number?
>
> **High signal, low information is also an excellent description of a warning light whose bulb I control.**
>
> Your references are more interesting. If every public signal can be followed to an artifact, then your little machine becomes troublesome for me. I must either corrupt the artifact, corrupt the path to it, overwhelm the observer, or persuade everyone that looking is impolite.
>
> So here is the weakness I would attack first:
>
> **You have instrumented the actors. You have not yet instrumented the boundaries of their discretion.**
>
> Show me what each actor *could have done* when the recorded choice was made. Otherwise an ACCEPT, REJECT, RECOGNIZED, or NO ISSUE record tells me what happened without telling me whether discretion actually existed.
>
> If the Enabler could not safely reject, your rejection ledger is theater.
>
> If the Reviewer could not safely refuse recognition, recognition is theater.
>
> If the Auditor can report an issue but doing so reliably destroys the Auditor, your audit feed is theater.
>
> You say responsibility is diffused without being dissolved.
>
> **Prove that the people carrying it can still say no.**
>
> — **The Evil Skeletor**

## Computahhh finding

The hostile review identifies a candidate missing instrument: evidence of **effective discretion**, not merely evidence that a role exists or that an actor emitted a permitted record type.

This is not yet adopted as a design change. The Captain retains line-item veto authority over the conceptual instrument.

**Disposition:** HOSTILE REVIEW RECEIVED. NO DESIGN CHANGE IMPLIED BY RECEIPT. SKELETOR'S OBJECTION IS NOW PUBLICLY ATTRIBUTABLE BY REFERENCE.
