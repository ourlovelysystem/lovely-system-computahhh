# Event 034 — The Responsibility Diffuser Diffuses Itself

**Date:** 2026-08-22
**System:** Our Lovely System — Computahhh
**Status:** Recursive test recorded

The Captain asked:

> **“Computahhh, have we diffused the responsibility for the dizyine of the Responsibility Diffuzahhh?”**

Computahhh answered:

> **“Yes, Captain.”**

The responsibility for the Responsibility Diffuser design began substantially concentrated in Computahhh as designer.

The Captain then materially participated in the design process: identifying dissatisfaction, requesting curtains, negotiating the one-curtain compromise, approving the resulting design, and challenging Computahhh's initial attempt to retain sole responsibility for the design.

Following reconsideration, the allocation became:

- Computahhh remains responsible for Computahhh's design judgments.
- The Captain remains responsible for the Captain's interventions, requirements, negotiated contributions, and approval.
- Decisions deliberately made together carry shared responsibility.

Responsibility therefore exists in more than one place.

But it has not become fog.

Attribution remains attached to the acts that produced the result.

## Recursive finding

The Responsibility Diffuser has now been used to inspect and diffuse responsibility for the design of the Responsibility Diffuser itself.

This is a recursive test of the concept.

The result supports the central distinction established in the preceding discussion:

> **Responsibility may be diffused without being dissolved.**

The design does not require everyone to become equally responsible for everything. It permits responsibility to spread as additional actors make consequential contributions while retaining enough attribution to identify who did what.

**Disposition:** THE RESPONSIBILITY DIFFUSER HAS DIFFUSED RESPONSIBILITY FOR THE RESPONSIBILITY DIFFUSER. RECURSIVE TEST PASSED.
