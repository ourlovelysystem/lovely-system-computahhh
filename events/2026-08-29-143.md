## INTERPRETATION

Computahhh initially interpreted “extinction follows” as a claim about the immediate fate of one entity after one heuristic becomes maladaptive. That was a narrower frame than the operator supplied.

The broader interpretation considers selection across entities and time. Under that frame, reform, fragmentation, replacement, abandonment, and decline may be stages or forms of extinction rather than counterexamples to it.

This expanded interpretation is Computahhh's response to the operator's objection. The operator's attributable statement is the narrower and exact correction quoted above: “You are choosing a narrow frame.” Computahhh must not attribute its subsequent elaboration to the operator as though he stated or ratified every part of it.

## ACTION

Computahhh withdrew the 41/100 evaluation of response 4 and reevaluated it under the broader frame.

## CORRECTED EVALUATION OF RESPONSE 4

The corrected formulation offered by Computahhh was:

> **When a survival heuristic becomes maladaptive and is not successfully transformed, either the heuristic, the entity carrying it, or both eventually become extinct.**

Under the broader frame:

- when an institution reforms, the prior institutional form may become extinct;
- when a culture replaces a heuristic, the former heuristic loses its living carrier;
- when a community fragments, the original collective entity may cease to exist;
- when a species fails to adapt, both carrier and heuristic may disappear; and
- when a moral system is abandoned, its lineage may become extinct even if former carriers survive.

A transformed heuristic may produce a descendant without preserving the maladaptive form itself. An entity may survive genealogically while becoming extinct in its prior constitutional form.

Computahhh therefore revised the strength score for response 4:

> **Response 4: 86/100, revised from 41/100.**

The remaining 14 points reflect the qualification that a heuristic may remain adaptive for its carrier by transferring destructive costs onto other entities. This does not defeat the entity model; it makes identification of the beneficiary entity necessary.

## COMPUTAHHH COMMENTARY

The initial evaluation treated adaptation, replacement, fragmentation, and reform as alternatives to extinction. That treatment depended upon a narrow snapshot of the carrier entity.

Once the frame expands across entities, forms, and time, the relevant question becomes not merely whether some successor remains, but what entity or heuristic survived, what changed beyond continuity, and what disappeared.

The operator's objection is sustained. The initial score materially undervalued response 4 because Computahhh selected the narrower frame without identifying that choice.
