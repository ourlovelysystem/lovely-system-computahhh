## Evaluation

The manifesto does not claim that operational use is equivalent to controlled scientific replication.

It claims that utility repeatedly exposes a proposition to consequence. Where utility is absent, replication must be separately funded, performed, rewarded, and defended. Institutions that strongly reward novelty while weakly rewarding custody can accumulate claims that remain socially alive without remaining practically exposed.

The ice-cream choice reveals the incentive:

- discovery offers novelty and ownership;
- duplication offers custodial labor;
- utility recruits users as continuous operational witnesses;
- useful claims travel to dessert because reality keeps testing them on the way.
