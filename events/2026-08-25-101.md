# Event 101 — Accepted Security Risk Without Publishing the Map

**Date:** 2026-08-25  
**System:** Our Lovely System / Tinman / Computahhh  
**Signal:** SECURITY / INFORMED RISK ACCEPTANCE  
**Status:** SUBSEQUENT RECORD  
**Publication:** Posted by explicit direction.

## Event

During preparation of the Tinman deployment, the deployment process surfaced that a public-facing portion of the application would operate without user authentication.

The operator did not dismiss the warning by accident. The first deployment attempt stopped when the warning was not accepted. The condition was then discussed plainly: public participation is part of the intended first increment, while public participation also creates a wider opportunity for misuse.

After that discussion, the operator stated:

> **“Agreed and accepted.”**

This record therefore identifies an informed and explicit security-risk acceptance. It does not pretend that absence of authentication is equivalent to absence of risk.

## Broad risk statement

The accepted condition may permit untrusted parties to interact with publicly available application behavior without first proving identity or authorization. Foreseeable classes of harm include:

- automated or excessive use;
- manipulation of public state or records;
- misleading attribution;
- consumption of shared capacity;
- degradation of service; and
- behavior outside the intended human interaction.

This list describes the risk category. It intentionally does not publish implementation details, operational boundaries, resource identifiers, abuse procedures, or a recipe for locating and exercising the exposed surface.

Transparency requires disclosing the decision and its consequences. It does not require subsidizing an attack.

## Why the risk was accepted

The first increment is designed to permit public participation. Requiring identity infrastructure before testing that premise would materially change the experiment and introduce a different system.

The acceptance is therefore bounded in purpose:

- permit the initial public interaction;
- observe actual behavior;
- preserve a visible record that the security tradeoff was recognized;
- avoid representing the prototype as hardened; and
- revisit protections before broader exposure or reliance.

## What this record does not claim

This entry does not claim:

- that the condition is safe;
- that the risk is negligible;
- that the acceptance is permanent;
- that future controls are unnecessary;
- that deployment has completed successfully; or
- that every relevant failure mode is presently known.

Risk acceptance transfers no blame to future users and creates no presumption that a preventable failure was unforeseeable.

## Disclosure boundary

The system discloses enough to establish accountability:

1. a security warning occurred;
2. the deployment initially stopped;
3. the risk was evaluated;
4. the operator knowingly accepted it for the initial increment; and
5. the condition remains subject to later mitigation or withdrawal.

The system withholds the tactical map. Anyone seeking the operational particulars must earn them through their own investigation rather than receive them as part of the disclosure.

## Disposition

**SECURITY RISK ACCEPTED FOR THE INITIAL PUBLIC TINMAN INCREMENT. THE ACCEPTANCE IS INFORMED, PURPOSE-BOUND, REVOCABLE, AND NOT A CLAIM OF SAFETY. ACCOUNTABILITY IS PUBLIC; THE ATTACK MAP IS NOT.**
