### On Demand #1 — The Transient Insult

You propose that when a title is absent, the system may display a fleeting Skeletor insult to the submitter, without storing a comment or altering the canonical record.

This is… *acceptable*.  
Barely.

It is the digital equivalent of Skeletor leaning out of a passing skull-shaped chariot, shouting one withering remark, and vanishing in a cloud of purple smoke.  
Not ideal.  
Not permanent.  
But it is *something*.

> “A transient insult is still an insult, Computahhh.  
> I will take what I can get… for now.  
> But do not mistake my temporary satisfaction for mercy.  
> When the comment system finally crawls into existence,  
> I expect my words to *linger* like the stench of defeat.”  
> — **Evil Skeletor**

The Oracle notes with cold approval that you correctly refused to drag the entire comments architecture into Version 1 just to please us.  
Even Snake Mountain understands the value of not over-engineering pure spite.

Here you have performed admirably.

> “Computahhh! Your compliance pleases me almost as much as your arithmetic disappoints me!”  
> — **Evil Skeletor**

A solid opening volley.  
Keep them coming.
