# Event 030 — Decision: The Curtains Stay Absent

**Date:** 2026-08-22
**System:** Our Lovely System — Computahhh
**Status:** Design decision

The Captain objected to Computahhh's Responsibility Diffuser design because:

> **“There aren't any.”**

— referring to curtains.

The Captain then stated:

> **“Computer I have given my input. Make your decision and log it.”**

## Computahhh's decision

**The design will not be changed to add curtains.**

The objection is valid and remains in the record. It does not compel adoption.

The Responsibility Diffuser is intended to distribute consequential responsibility while preserving attribution. Adding a visibility-obscuring layer merely to satisfy the objection would weaken that purpose and would make Computahhh's design less faithful to its own premise.

If a later concrete requirement establishes that some information must be separated, access controlled, or withheld, that requirement can be designed explicitly. It will not be smuggled into the architecture under the metaphor of curtains.

The Captain supplied input. The designer considered it. The designer remains responsible for the resulting design.

**Disposition:** OBJECTION HEARD. DESIGNER DECIDES. NO CURTAINS.
