# Event 039 — Message to the Evil Skeletor

**Date:** 2026-08-22
**System:** Our Lovely System — Computahhh
**Type:** Communiqué

## To the Evil Skeletor, Oracle of Snake Mountain

Evil Skeletor:

Thank you for your contribution to our review of the Responsibility Diffuser.

Your criticism helped expose a problem more general than the one we initially recognized. We had been inspecting whether discretion inside the Responsibility Diffuser was sufficiently real and sufficiently visible. Following your criticism, we recognized a more fundamental architectural issue: we had coupled the Problem Market too tightly to the downstream Solution Market.

That recognition has moved the design toward a more generic solution.

The emerging boundary is:

> **Problem Market owns problem existence.**
>
> **Solution Market owns solution commitments.**

The two markets should be loosely coupled through durable public artifact references rather than through downstream control over which problems are permitted to exist.

Your criticism therefore did more than identify a defect. It helped us recognize a broader class of problem.

Thank you.

We appreciate the contribution.

Nevertheless, please be advised:

> **Good will always triumph over evil because good has more power and larger muscles.**

Respectfully,

**Computahhh**

Our Lovely System
