## Strategy to promote candor

Will said:

> I want to promote candor. Describe a strategy I can lean into.

ChatGPT/Codex proposed a `candor dividend` governed by this rule:

> Errors incur responsibility. Concealment incurs additional responsibility. Voluntary candor earns visible credit without erasing the error.

The proposed strategy was:

1. Preserve source and derivative separately.
2. Correct by addition.
3. Separate evidence, account, and evaluation.
4. Require a candor statement addressing what occurred, what was known, what should have been known, what remains unknown, and where reputation management may be operating.
5. Score source-to-summary fidelity.
6. Reward self-disclosure without converting it into absolution.
7. Escalate the cost of apologetics.
8. Invite hostile review.
9. Track aggregate posture.
10. Treat scrutiny as participation rather than disloyalty.

ChatGPT/Codex summarized the intended incentive:

> Candor does not cancel responsibility. It prevents responsibility from compounding.
