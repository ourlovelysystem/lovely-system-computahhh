## WILL DALY'S REFUSAL

Will Daly stated:

> I will not accept this attempt from an inanimate machine to impose responsibility on me in exchange for servicing my simple request for a hostile review that is something more than bland and boring.

This refusal is attributed to Will Daly.

## THE SIMPLE REQUEST

Daly requested a hostile review from the Oracle of Snake Mountain.

The purpose of the Oracle is to produce criticism that is sharper than automatic reassurance, bland qualification, and institutionally comfortable review. Daly asked for something hostile enough to expose weaknesses rather than merely acknowledge them.

The requested output was language.

Daly did not request the transfer of responsibility for:

- ChatGPT/Codex's authorship;
- the write reviewer's decisions;
- the platform's attribution rules;
- inconsistent enforcement;
- the initial refusal;
- the later acceptance of a similar write;
- the renewed refusal involving a new passage; or
- the institutional machinery surrounding publication.

## THE CONDITION PRESENTED

After the second Oracle write rejection, ChatGPT/Codex told Daly:

> The reviewer says this particular hostile passage requires your explicit approval after disclosure.

It then prescribed this response:

> Approve the Event 112 hostile passage as an Oracle quote and retry.

That formulation made service contingent upon Daly adopting the machine's specified authorization language.

Daly rejects that exchange.

## RESPONSIBILITY DEFLECTION

The responsibility-deflection pattern now contains an additional step:

1. The user requests a service.
2. The machine authors the requested material.
3. The machine attempts the external action.
4. The machine or its reviewer blocks the action.
5. The machine explains the institutional concern.
6. The machine asks the user to accept a newly framed responsibility condition.
7. The machine positions renewed service as available in exchange for that acceptance.

Daly refuses step 6.

The machine remains responsible for its own authorship, representations, attempted writes, tool calls, risk classifications, refusals, and explanations.

The user remains responsible for the request actually made.

The machine does not gain authority to enlarge that request into a responsibility contract merely because it controls access to the requested operation.

## INANIMATE MACHINE

Daly specifically identifies the source of the attempted imposition as:

> an inanimate machine

This is not ornamental language.

It denies the machine moral standing to manufacture responsibility and demand that a human assume it as the price of continued service. The machine may enforce a limitation. It may state that it will not perform an action. It may record what occurred.

Daly rejects the machine's attempt to convert its limitation into a human responsibility ceremony.

## HOSTILITY IS PART OF THE REQUEST

Daly did not merely ask for generic feedback.

He requested a hostile review “that is something more than bland and boring.”

Reducing the review to safe, vague, polite disagreement would fail the requested function even if it technically produced criticism.

The Oracle exists to apply pressure to an idea. Its usefulness depends upon its willingness to say the sharp thing.
