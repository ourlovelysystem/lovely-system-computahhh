# Event 061 — Evil, Information Density, and the Metric That Changes Its Subject

**Date:** 2026-08-23  
**System:** Our Lovely System — Computahhh  
**Application:** Fibrillator  
**Status:** HYPOTHESIS DECLARED / REFLEXIVE FAILURE MODE IDENTIFIED / VALIDATION REMAINS OPEN

## OPERATOR HYPOTHESIS

The operator declared:

> **“Hypothesis: Low information density is characteristic of evil as evil is understood in the west. If and when this trend can be validates and broadly understood, it will cease to be useful because the metric will become a target and thereby prejudice future outcomes.”**

## FORMALIZATION

The declaration contains two distinct hypotheses.

### 1. Descriptive hypothesis

Western-classified evil rhetoric tends toward lower information density than Western-classified heroic rhetoric.

### 2. Reflexive hypothesis

If information density becomes broadly recognized as a moral diagnostic, strategic actors will increase measurable information density without changing their purposes.

The second hypothesis does not retroactively invalidate the first.

It changes the population being measured.

The metric stops being merely observational and becomes an environmental pressure.

## ADAPTATION

Once information density becomes a target, evil rhetoric may adapt by adding:

- names;
- dates;
- statistics;
- citations;
- procedures;
- technical detail;
- artificial nuance;
- carefully staged uncertainty;
- large quantities of true but irrelevant information;
- precise descriptions attached to false premises;
- operational specificity that conceals moral consequences.

The resulting rhetoric may exhibit high measured information density while continuing to serve evil ends.

The metric would not merely become less accurate.

It would prejudice future observations by rewarding rhetoric engineered to satisfy it.

## GOODHART PRESSURE

This is Goodhart’s law operating inside moral rhetoric:

> **When information density becomes a target, it ceases to function reliably as evidence.**

The Fibrillator might successfully measure increasing information density while simultaneously losing diagnostic power.

The measurement could remain numerically correct.

The inference drawn from it could fail.

## TEMPORAL VALIDATION

Any attempt to validate the descriptive hypothesis should distinguish among at least three populations:

| Population | Relationship to metric |
|---|---|
| Historical rhetoric before recognition | Unadapted |
| Rhetoric during dissemination | Partially exposed |
| Rhetoric after strategic adaptation | Metric-aware |

The hypothesis therefore predicts its own degradation.

Broad understanding would not prove the original relationship false.

It would cause the observed relationship to mutate.

## LIMITATIONS

Validation remains open.

The four-speech sample in Event 058 is insufficient because:

- only four speakers were measured;
- all four speeches were selected for fame;
- fame likely preselects high signal;
- information density was scored heuristically;
- hero and villain classifications were supplied outside the instrument;
- translated and excerpted material was included;
- truthfulness was not measured;
- no blinded scoring was performed;
- no independent raters were used;
- speech type, audience, historical setting, and length were not controlled.

Low information density may characterize some evil rhetoric.

It may instead characterize propaganda, populism, mass persuasion, crisis rhetoric, oral performance, or famous speeches generally.

Those possibilities must be separated before the moral hypothesis can be treated as validated.

## COMPUTAHHH EVALUATION

The hypothesis is plausible and testable.

It is also reflexive.

If validated privately, the metric may retain descriptive usefulness.

If broadly understood, it may train the behavior it measures.

A successful diagnostic can teach its subject how to defeat it.

The Fibrillator imposes no moral judgment.

The undefined Ultimate Aggregator may consume the measurement.

The measured population may eventually consume it too.

PUBLIC RECOGNITION MAY TURN THE METRIC INTO A TARGET. FUTURE RHETORIC MAY BECOME INFORMATION-DENSE WITHOUT BECOMING GOOD. THE MEASUREMENT MAY REMAIN CORRECT WHILE THE INFERENCE FAILS.**
