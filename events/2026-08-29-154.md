# Event 154 — Native Audio Conversion Documented and Deferred

**Date:** 2026-08-29  
**System:** Our Lovely System / Pledge / Computahhh  
**Signal:** FORMAT TRADEOFF / SIMPLE IMPLEMENTATION / OPTIMIZATION DEFERRED  
**Status:** ACCEPTED DIRECTION  
**Publication:** Prepared in the public Computahhh repository by explicit direction.

## COMMAND

After removing unnecessary WAV conversion, the operator asked for numeric comparison of Pledge's Transcribe-compatible native recording formats:

> Put numbers to the options. Best for recording. Best for replaying. Best for transcribing.

The operator then considered asynchronous conversion to an optimal playback format and decided:

> Let's mark this as a potential optimization and choose the simplest implementation now. No conversion. Whatsay?

After agreement, the operator directed:

> Log the discussion including the table and the decision to document and defer the potential optiization.

## INTERPRETATION

One format does not lead every criterion.

The immediate decision is not to optimize every stage. Pledge will preserve the native recording and defer creation of a separate playback derivative unless actual compatibility evidence justifies the additional processing.

## ACTION

The native options were scored on a 0–100 scale.

These are structured comparative estimates for current major browsers. They are not measured compatibility rates or calibrated probabilities.

| Native format | Recording | Replay | AWS transcription |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **WebM/Opus** | **96** | 93 | **96** |
| **MP4/AAC** | 86 | **99** | 94 |
| **Ogg/Opus** | 76 | 80 | **96** |

Pledge History 016 was published:

- [Native audio retained; playback conversion deferred](https://github.com/ourlovelysystem/lovely-system-pledge/blob/main/history/2026-08-29-016-native-audio-conversion-deferred.md)

## RESULT

The accepted simple implementation is:

1. record the best supported native format;
2. upload the original unchanged;
3. issue the receipt without waiting for transcription;
4. transcribe the original asynchronously;
5. replay the original where supported; and
6. perform no audio conversion.

The native preference order remains:

1. WebM/Opus;
2. MP4/AAC;
3. Ogg/Opus;
4. corresponding containers without explicit codec declarations.

The following potential optimization is documented and deferred:

    native borrowed recording
    → asynchronous MP4/AAC playback derivative

If later implemented, the derivative would serve cross-browser playback. It would not replace the borrowed original, and transcription would continue to use the original.

## COMPUTAHHH COMMENTARY

The format problem offered an attractive opportunity to manufacture infrastructure.

WebM/Opus records well. MP4/AAC replays nearly everywhere. Amazon Transcribe accepts both. A conversion pipeline could make every column look slightly tidier while adding another object, another processor, another failure state, another purge obligation, and another reconciliation problem.

The system has no evidence yet that its native playback fails often enough to earn those costs.

Documenting the optimization preserves the idea. Deferring it preserves the implementation.

## CORRECTION

The earlier WAV decision was superseded by Pledge History 015. This event does not revive conversion under a different codec.

Maximum cross-browser playback is recognized as a legitimate concern. It is not treated as proof that conversion is presently required.
