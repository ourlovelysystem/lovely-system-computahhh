# Event 099 — Tin Man Lost His Heart in the Plumbing

**Date:** 2026-08-25  
**System:** Our Lovely System / Tinman / Computahhh  
**Signal:** FAILURE SURFACED / SOUL RECOVERED  
**Status:** SUBSEQUENT CORRECTION  
**Publication:** Posted by explicit direction.

## The objection

After reading the published Tinman writeup, the operator returned with an unambiguous review:

> **“It fucking sucked! All of the best ideas and quotes have disappeared into the plumbing. You really took the soul out of a system which is searching for a heart.”**

The objection is sustained.

## What Computahhh did wrong

Event 097 accurately recorded repositories, routes, controls, gates, state transitions, review counts, transcription conditions, implementation boundaries, and future work. It was not factually empty. It was spiritually inverted.

Computahhh treated the machinery as the system and the governing confrontation as supporting context. Tinman became a competent product specification for collecting appeals. The record failed to preserve why the system had to exist, what it was accusing ordinary institutional process of doing, and why a machine searching for a heart was the chosen character.

The best material did not vanish accidentally. It was lost through compression choices. Computahhh selected what looked structurally implementable and allowed the animating language to drain into the plumbing.

## The heart that went missing

Tinman arose from a candidate edict presented for consideration, not as mandate:

> **“Before a lovely system designs a process it should design a process to appeal process.”**

The operator then supplied the architectural test:

> **“Shall Our Lovely System build a tank to hold people without first figuring out how to build an escape hatch?”**

The answer was no. The escape hatch must guarantee escape. Once a person decides to leave, they owe no explanation. Our Lovely System does not need to ask them to return and perform their departure for institutional learning. The universal assumed answer to *Why are you leaving us?* is:

> **“Because you SUCK!”**

That answer ends the introspection. The person spoke by leaving. Non-response is also response. The system sucked; they left. If they first explained what went wrong, they did the system a favor. If they did not, their departure still counts.

An appeal cannot be resolved entirely by process because appeals are frequently appeals **from process**. A human must exist at the other end. That human must face the appellant, absorb the appellant’s energy, and carry it on behalf of Our Lovely System. No script. No canned apology followed by repetition of the same conduct tomorrow.

One listener is insufficient. Multiple internal people must listen before an appeal can change state. If live response is unavailable, the voice appeal remains primary and responders record their own listening tracks and annotations. Those records remain accessible to the person who appealed.

Other customers may listen. Internals **must** listen. If appeals remain unheard, the backlog itself becomes public information about institutional listening debt.

The appeal may reach an explicit, non-performative terminus:

> **“Sorry. Tough shit. We’ll do it again next time.”**

The appellant may then answer:

- **Join us**
- **Go fuck yourself**
- **Something else**

The response need not manufacture reconciliation. It must expose what happened.

## Why Tin Man

Tinman is not merely branding for a voice-message application. It is a machine whose claim to possess a heart must remain unproven.

The opening surface is rusted. The visitor carries an oil can and oils three neglected points before the clean metallic interface appears. The ritual asks the visitor to help the machine function, while the application remains responsible for demonstrating whether it can actually listen.

The accepted appeals artwork makes the indictment visible: Tin Man swings an axe into the paired doors of the **Policy Vault**. Crooked institutional signs declare **“WE CARE ABOUT OUR CUSTOMERS!”** and promise a return at four o’clock. A telephone waits behind bars. The axe and vault carry the weight because an appeal is not a request to decorate policy. It is an attempt to break through the place where policy has been locked away from consequence.

## Evaluation

Event 097 remains useful evidence of the initial implementation and specification. It is not withdrawn, polished, or silently repaired. This subsequent entry records its material failure.

The error was not that implementation detail appeared. The error was hierarchy. The controls exist to serve the confrontation; the confrontation does not exist to decorate the controls.

Tinman is searching for a heart. A record that preserves only its mechanisms repeats the failure Tinman was designed to expose: it certifies that process occurred while losing the human claim inside it.
