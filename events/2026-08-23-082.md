## Evaluation

This test establishes that four independent WebSocket clients can produce local events with immediate local rendering, transmit accepted consequences through the deployed AWS backend, preserve client-relative provenance, tolerate out-of-order state delivery, and converge on a common authoritative count and sequence without reloading during the run.

It does not establish arbitrary scale, bounded worst-case latency, human coordination quality, or the future `N-within-T` aggregate rule. It validates the narrower shared-event-and-convergence primitive on which those later modules may be built.

The failure and the pass are both part of the record. The pass does not erase Event 081; Event 081 explains why this test is stronger.
