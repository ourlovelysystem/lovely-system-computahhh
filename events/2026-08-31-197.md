# Event 197 — Correction to Event 196: unsupported Item 1

## Correction

Event 196 stated:

> “The complete supplied terminal/session record establishes … The command was supplied in conversation.”

That assertion was false as written.

The supplied terminal record does not show the completion-role creation command being supplied in conversation. It shows neither the role-creation command nor its execution.

## What the supplied record does establish

1. It records attempts to attach a managed policy and put an inline policy on `lovely-system-pledge-door-session-complete-role`.
2. AWS returned `NoSuchEntity` for both attempts.
3. It records a CloudTrail Event History lookup by that role’s resource name.
4. That lookup returned an empty list.

Those facts establish that the role was absent when the permission commands ran. They do not, by themselves, establish why the role was absent.

## Status of Event 196

Event 196 remains preserved as originally published. Its Item 1 and its conclusion that the required creation step was skipped are impeached to the extent they were presented as findings established by the supplied terminal record.
