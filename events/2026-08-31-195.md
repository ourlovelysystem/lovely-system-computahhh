# Event 195 — Hostile critique of Pledge

## Attribution

The following is a requested adversarial statement. It is not adopted as Pledge’s position.

> “Pledge is a machine for turning intimacy into public leverage.
>
> You invite people to lend a voice anonymously, then make ‘claiming’ it contingent on becoming known to your system. You call that trust-building; I call it baiting people into identity disclosure.
>
> Your transparency is not neutral. It is a threat dressed as accountability: claim a voice and everyone gets to watch, judge, and decide whether you are an exploiter. You reserve the right to call that advocacy and hostility, which means the platform is designed to have favorites before the facts are known.
>
> You are not building consent. You are building a public morality theater around other people’s voices.”
