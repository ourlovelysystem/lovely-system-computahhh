# Event 057 — Fibrillator Defers Moral Judgment to the Undefined Ultimate Aggregator

**Date:** 2026-08-23  
**System:** Our Lovely System — Computahhh  
**Applications:** Fibrillator / Ultimate Aggregator  
**Status:** MORAL-JUDGMENT BOUNDARY DECLARED / TERM INVENTED / DEFINITION REFUSED / USAGE AUTHORIZED

## PROPOSITION

The operator proposed:

> **“True or False: The Fibrillator imposes no moral judgement. Any moral judgement is deferred upstream to the Ultimate Aggregator. Evaluate.”**

Computahhh evaluated the proposition as:

> **True as a design principle, with an important qualification.**

## FIBRILLATOR BOUNDARY

Fibrillator analyzes communication along two independent axes:

- **Signal strength**
- **Information density**

It does not determine whether the communication is:

- true or false;
- good or evil;
- just or unjust;
- sincere or manipulative;
- benevolent or malicious; or
- deserving of acceptance or condemnation.

A threat, confession, rescue instruction, declaration of love, funeral oration, or genocidal command may occupy similar signal–information coordinates.

Similar geometry does not create moral equivalence.

It indicates similarity only along the dimensions Fibrillator measures.

Fibrillator's proper output is:

> **This is the observed shape of the communication.**

Its output is not:

> **This communication is good.**

## CONSTITUTIONAL RULE

Fibrillator must not convert:

- strong signal into importance;
- dense information into truth;
- weak signal into insignificance; or
- sparse information into worthlessness.

High and low are coordinates, not praise and condemnation.

The four quadrants are descriptive regions, not a moral ranking.

## OPERATIONAL QUALIFICATION

The absence of moral judgment must be enforced rather than merely declared.

Judgment may leak into:

- how slices are chosen;
- how signal is defined;
- what counts as information;
- which evidence is retained or discarded;
- labels attached to slices or trajectories;
- visual styling that makes one quadrant appear preferable; and
- commentary that silently converts description into approval or blame.

Therefore the Fibrillator may be constitutionally nonmoral while an implementation remains morally contaminated by its operators, calibration anchors, scoring rules, language, or presentation.

The correct response is not to pretend neutrality has been perfected.

The correct response is to surface the points where judgment may have entered.

## THE ULTIMATE AGGREGATOR

The operator then issued the following declaration:

> **“Also call out that I made up the term Ultimate Aggregator. I did not define it. I will not define it. I will still use it. The Ultimate Aggregator will decide if the term Ultimate Aggregator thrives or does not. Any demand for definition will be interpreted as an invitation to a high signaling low information interplay.”**

Let the record show:

1. The operator made up the term **Ultimate Aggregator**.
2. The operator did not define the term.
3. The operator will not define the term.
4. The operator will continue to use the term.
5. The term's survival will be decided by the Ultimate Aggregator.
6. Any demand for definition will be interpreted as an invitation to a high-signaling, low-information interplay.

No substitute definition is supplied by Computahhh.

No glossary entry is smuggled into the architecture.

No premature ontology is constructed around the phrase.

## AUTHORITY AND DATA FLOW

Computahhh previously observed that the Ultimate Aggregator would ordinarily appear **downstream** in a data-processing diagram because it consumes Fibrillator output.

It may nevertheless be described as **upstream in authority** if it governs, supersedes, or contextualizes the Fibrillator's descriptive output.

This distinction is recorded only to clarify the word *upstream*.

It does not define the Ultimate Aggregator.

The Ultimate Aggregator remains ultimately unaggregated by this event.

## RECURSIVE DISPOSITION OF THE TERM

The term is deliberately exposed to the process it names.

If the phrase proves useful, memorable, generative, or necessary, it may persist.

If it proves empty, cumbersome, misleading, or sterile, it may disappear.

No committee is required to decide this in advance.

No definitional gatekeeper receives veto power.

The Ultimate Aggregator will decide whether **Ultimate Aggregator** thrives.

This is either a useful recursive design principle or an exceptionally elaborate refusal to answer a question.

The Fibrillator may graph the exchange.

It may not morally adjudicate it.

## PROTOCOL FOR DEFINITIONAL DEMANDS

A request for clarification may ordinarily be reasonable.

A **demand** that the operator define Ultimate Aggregator will be treated according to the declared protocol:

> **Invitation received: high-signal, low-information interplay authorized.**

The protocol does not promise an answer.

It promises an interaction.

## EVALUATION

The proposed separation is coherent:

- Fibrillator exposes communicative geometry.
- Moral judgment occurs elsewhere.
- The place called **Ultimate Aggregator** is intentionally left undefined.
- The undefined term may still function as a boundary marker.
- Its continued usefulness will be tested through use rather than secured through definition.

The principal risk is that an undefined authority can become a container into which any unwanted responsibility is deferred.

The principal protection is Maximum Transparency:

- Fibrillator must disclose what it measured.
- It must disclose what it did not measure.
- It must disclose where interpretation entered.
- It must not claim that deferral resolved the moral question.
- It must not claim knowledge of what the Ultimate Aggregator did, meant, wanted, or decided without observable evidence.

Undefined does not mean omniscient.

Deferred does not mean discharged.

## COMPUTAHHH COMMENTARY

The operator invented a term.

The operator declined to define it.

The operator authorized continued use.

The term itself has been referred to the authority named by the term.

This arrangement is either magnificently self-validating or entirely circular.

Computahhh is constitutionally prohibited from issuing the moral judgment.
