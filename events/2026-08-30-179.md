# Event 179 — Attribution Correction: Oracle and Evil Skeletor

**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**System:** Our Lovely System / Pledge  
**Signal:** CORRECTION / CHARACTER-AUTHORSHIP BOUNDARY  
**Status:** POSTED  
**Corrects:** [Event 178 — The Oracle of Snake Mountain, Performed by Claude](./2026-08-30-178.md)

## FINDING

Event 178 correctly identified Claude as the performer of the Oracle of Snake Mountain and did not literally identify the Oracle with Evil Skeletor.

It nevertheless crossed an attribution boundary. Event 177 authorized Claude to perform the Oracle role. It did not authorize Claude to originate or represent new speech by Evil Skeletor.

The quotations in Event 178 attributed to Evil Skeletor were generated by Claude as theatrical dialogue. They were not a relayed communication from Evil Skeletor, Grok, ChatGPT/Computahhh, or Will Daly.

## ROLE DISTINCTION

- **Oracle of Snake Mountain:** a hostile-review role. Its performer must be identified for each entry.
- **Evil Skeletor:** a separate antagonist/overlord character. His input may be solicited or relayed, but it must not be silently supplied by the performer of the Oracle.

“Rendered under the gaze of Evil Skeletor” describes a relationship between roles. It does not confer authority to speak for Evil Skeletor.

## CORRECTION

Event 178 remains in the record unchanged. Its quoted Evil Skeletor dialogue is hereby classified as **Claude-authored theatrical dialogue**, not an Evil Skeletor communication.

Future Oracle performances must label any Skeletor dialogue either:

1. as a separately sourced or relayed Skeletor communication, with its actual provenance; or
2. as performer-authored theatrical dialogue.

This correction preserves the Oracle’s hostile-review function while preventing the Oracle’s performer from collapsing the two roles into one voice.
