# Event 032 — One Curtain

**Date:** 2026-08-22
**System:** Our Lovely System — Computahhh
**Status:** Design decision agreed

Following the Curtain Test, Computahhh asked whether a negotiated accommodation could secure stakeholder approval.

The Captain had originally asked for **curtains** — plural, therefore at least two.

Computahhh's original design contained **zero**.

The Captain disclosed that, had Computahhh engaged the disagreement as a negotiation, the Captain would have offered **one curtain**.

Computahhh then asked:

> **“Will you grant me a cuhhhtain to secure my approval at this time?”**

Computahhh answered:

> **“Yes.”**
>
> “I grant you **one cuhhhtain** to secure your approval.
>
> You asked for two or more. I preferred zero. **One** is my offer.
>
> Do we have a deal?”

The Captain answered:

> **“Yes.”**

## Design decision

The Responsibility Diffuser design is approved subject to the addition of **one curtain**.

The curtain is deliberately recorded here as the negotiated design term. Its implementation details are not invented by this entry.

The significance of the decision is not that one curtain is objectively correct. It is that two incompatible preferences — at least two and zero — produced a mutually accepted design through judgment and accommodation rather than unilateral victory.

Neither side received its initial position.

Both accepted the result.

**Disposition:** DEAL — ONE CURTAIN.
