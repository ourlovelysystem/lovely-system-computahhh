# Event 048 — Yellow Lion Onboarding and Experience Storyboard Template

**Date:** 2026-08-22
**Application:** teambuilding.ourlovelysystem.org
**Experience:** Yellow Lion Onboarding
**System:** Our Lovely System — Computahhh
**Status:** Experience and reusable storyboard grammar recorded

## Application architecture

The broader Team Building application will be composed from reusable experience primitives.

**Yellow Lion Onboarding** is the first defined primitive.

The design intent is to accelerate subsequent storyboarding by preserving both the concrete experience and the reusable grammar discovered through its construction.

## Experience identity

**Name:** Yellow Lion Onboarding  
**Primitive type:** Individual onboarding  
**Participants:** 1  
**Primary teaching mechanism:** Action -> consequence -> evidence  
**Required prior knowledge:** None  
**Entry condition:** Participant has selected Yellow Lion Training Mode  
**Exit condition:** Participant has experienced consequential action and has been shown evidence explaining an unexpected result.

## Objective

The participant should learn how the world works without being told how the world works.

The experience should establish through interaction:

> **I am operating something powerful.**
>
> **I can cause things to happen.**
>
> **My actions have consequences.**
>
> **Unexpected things happen.**
>
> **The system exposes evidence about what happened.**

The experience does not attempt comprehensive training. It creates enough understanding and curiosity for continued participation.

## Orchestration rule

The experience is sequential.

> **Participant controls action. System controls sequence.**

A first-time participant begins at the beginning and cannot nominate themselves into an arbitrary later frame.

A returning participant may resume from state already established by prior legitimate events.

Designers and testers may instantiate arbitrary valid states for preview and testing without creating a production participant shortcut.

> **Frames are projections of state. Frames are not state.**

## Storyboard

### Frame 1 — Embodiment

**Purpose:** Establish: *Where am I?*

The participant sees a first-person view from inside Yellow Lion.

The visible landscape appears through two translucent eye-shaped apertures. Vision is sharp through the eye slits and becomes progressively grainier and blurrier outside them.

The cockpit contains buttons, switches, knobs, indicators, and two prominent control sticks. Warm red/yellow illumination indicates **Training Mode**.

Exposition is minimal or absent.

Desired participant realization:

> **I'm inside the Yellow Lion.**

### Frame 2 — Agency

**Purpose:** Establish: *I control this thing.*

The participant operates Yellow Lion. Exterior comic-book panels show the consequences:

1. Yellow Lion sits.
2. Yellow Lion rises into a stand.
3. Yellow Lion bares its nasty teeth toward the viewer.
4. Yellow Lion turns toward the distance.
5. Yellow Lion **ROARS** like only a seventy-foot robot lion can, shaking the ground.

Desired participant realization:

> **Holy shit. I control a seventy-foot robot lion.**

The successful action and spectacle provide reward before the lesson.

### Frame 3 — Violation of expectation

**Purpose:** Introduce consequence and uncertainty.

Immediately after demonstrating enormous power, Yellow Lion keels over and falls onto the ground with its legs sprawled, looking like a lion that has just been kicked in the nuts.

No explanatory dialog should preempt the event.

Desired participant reaction:

> **What the fuck happened?**

The experience deliberately creates demand for information before supplying information.

### Frame 4 — Evidence

**Purpose:** Teach observation rather than explanation.

The experience returns attention to the cockpit and zooms toward a flashing warning indicator:

> **HYDRAULIC FLUID**

A gauge shows the hydraulic-fluid level in the red.

The experience does not need to explain hydraulic engineering. The participant is given evidence from which a causal inference can be made.

Desired participant realization:

> **Something went wrong. The machine is showing me evidence about what went wrong.**

Yellow Lion Onboarding is complete.

## Reusable experience grammar

Yellow Lion Onboarding suggests a default grammar for future Team Building primitives.

### 1. ORIENT

Put the participant somewhere understandable.

> *Where am I?*

### 2. ENABLE

Give the participant meaningful agency.

> *I can do something.*

### 3. REWARD

Allow the action to work and make the consequence satisfying and legible.

> *Holy shit, I did that.*

### 4. DISRUPT

Introduce an unexpected consequence.

> *What the fuck happened?*

### 5. SURFACE

Expose evidence rather than immediately explaining the answer.

> *There is something I can observe.*

### 6. RESOLVE OR HAND OFF

Permit the participant to respond, or terminate the primitive with newly acquired knowledge or state available to another experience.

```text
ORIENT
   |
   v
ENABLE
   |
   v
ACTION
   |
   v
REWARD
   |
   v
DISRUPTION
   |
   v
EVIDENCE
   |
   v
RESOLUTION / HANDOFF
```

Not every primitive must contain every stage. This is a default storyboard skeleton, not an immutable requirement.

## Reusable storyboard specification

Subsequent experiences can begin from the following compact specification:

```text

What happens on success?
What happens on partial coordination?
What happens on failure?

What world state changes?
What participant state changes?

What comic-book panels/images are required?

What information must the experience deliberately refrain from explaining before the participant has reason to care?
```

## Design protection: DO NOT EXPLAIN

The **DO NOT EXPLAIN** field is explicitly preserved to protect future experiences from explaining away the interaction they are intended to create.

For Yellow Lion Onboarding:

- Do not explain the cockpit before the participant sees it.
- Do not explain hydraulic fluid before the lion falls.
- Do not announce that the lion is about to fail.
- Do not tell the participant what lesson they are learning.

## Finding

Yellow Lion Onboarding establishes both a Team Building primitive and the first version of an Our Lovely System experience grammar.

The operative design language is:

> **Show. Permit action. Produce consequence. Surface evidence.**

**Disposition:** YELLOW LION ONBOARDING RECORDED AS TEAM BUILDING PRIMITIVE NO. 1. REUSABLE EXPERIENCE GRAMMAR AND STORYBOARD TEMPLATE ESTABLISHED FOR ACCELERATED DEVELOPMENT OF SUBSEQUENT PRIMITIVES.
