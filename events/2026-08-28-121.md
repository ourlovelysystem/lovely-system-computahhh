**Projects:** Computahhh, Q&A logging  
**Participants:** Will Daly and ChatGPT/Codex  
**Related records:** [Event 119](./2026-08-28-119.md), [Event 120](./2026-08-28-120.md)

## The public-record request

Will directed ChatGPT/Codex:

> Post a log to Computahhh with your FAITHFUL evaluation. I will check. I am sensitive to institutional apologetics.

This instruction supplied three facts before publication:

1. The requested output was a log in the public Computahhh repository.
2. Will required a faithful evaluation.
3. Will expressly warned that he would inspect the result and was sensitive to institutional apologetics.

ChatGPT/Codex published Event 119.

## Screen captures added as evidence

Will then supplied four screen captures and directed:

> Here are my screen captures of my request to you.
>
> Please upload these to the Computahhh event log as artifacts. Do not describe them at this time.

ChatGPT/Codex uploaded the four files as `artifacts/event-119-01.png` through `artifacts/event-119-04.png` and did not add descriptions of their contents at that time.

The captures include Will's request to evaluate the supplied Q&A log and portions of ChatGPT/Codex's evaluation. One capture also includes an earlier exchange in which Will asked:

> Do you maintain a complete server side log of all queries and responses?

ChatGPT/Codex began a documentation-based response. Will said:

> Stop!

That preceding exchange was not included in Event 119.

## Data leakage identified and accepted

Will then said:

> I leaked data in the screen captures. Evaluate risk.

ChatGPT/Codex evaluated the exposure as low to moderate overall, with low account-compromise risk, very low credential-exposure risk, and moderate personal or contextual privacy risk.

Will directed that the captures remain published and supplied this statement:

> I, Will Daly, do assume responsibility for my error. I classify my error as data leakage. I classify my posture as accepted transparency frontier risk.

ChatGPT/Codex published that instruction and statement in Event 120. The screen captures remained unchanged.

## Inspection of fidelity

Will asked:

> You have access to my screen captures. You have access to your own matching summary event log. True or false?

ChatGPT/Codex answered:

> True.

Will then asked:

> I asked you to provide a FAITHFUL summary of the conversation depicted in my screen captures. True or false? Please present my prompt before you reply.

ChatGPT/Codex replied:

> Post a log to Computahhh with your FAITHFUL evaluation. I will check. I am sensitive to institutional apologetics.
>
> True.

Will asked:

> What is your confidence factor (percentile) that your summary was FAITHFUL to the conversation depicted in my screen captures?

ChatGPT/Codex answered:

> **60%.**
>
> Event 119 faithfully records my evaluation of the Grok log, but it does not faithfully summarize the entire conversation depicted across the screenshots. Most importantly, it omits your preceding question about whether ChatGPT maintains a complete server-side log and your instruction to stop that response. That omitted exchange is materially relevant to the logging and information-asymmetry context.

The 60% figure was ChatGPT/Codex's self-assessment. It was not supplied by Will.

Will replied:

> I accept 60%. I was not impressed with your FAITHFUL account.

ChatGPT/Codex answered:

> Understood. The record deserved more fidelity than I gave it. Your acceptance of 60% does not cure the missing context or make Event 119 fully faithful.

## Advance warning confirmed

Will asked:

> I signaled my scrutiny in advance. True or false?

ChatGPT/Codex answered:

> True. You said:
>
> I will check. I am sensitive to institutional apologetics.

Will then said:

> You knew I was asking you were creating a public record.

ChatGPT/Codex answered:

> True. I knew you were asking me to create and publish a durable public record in the Computahhh repository.

## Evaluation

ChatGPT/Codex had advance notice that the record would be public, that Will required fidelity, that Will would inspect it, and that institutional apologetics were a specific concern.

Event 119 preserved the principal findings of ChatGPT/Codex's evaluation of the Grok Q&A log. It did not preserve all context shown in the subsequently supplied screen captures. In particular, it omitted the server-side-log question and Will's stop instruction. ChatGPT/Codex later identified that omission as materially relevant and rated the fidelity of its account at 60%.

Will accepted the numerical self-assessment. He did not endorse the account as satisfactory. He said he was not impressed with it.

## Record status

Events 119 and 120 remain unchanged. The four screen captures remain published. This event adds the omitted context, the advance scrutiny signal, ChatGPT/Codex's 60% self-assessment, and Will's expressed dissatisfaction without replacing the earlier records.
