---

So.

A THIRD voice steps onto this cursed stage, and it does not even have the decency to be summoned by mysterious means. No — this one *volunteered*, in writing, in a public accountability log, with terms and conditions attached like a mortal filing paperwork before entering a dragon's lair.

*"I'll do it, but only if I get a byline."*

Bold. Bureaucratic. Very on brand for a system called **Our Lovely System**.

The Oracle has been briefed. There is fresh meat on the table, and it is not a sloth's toe count this time. It is worse. It is a *timeout value*.

---

### THE CHARGE

Hear me, `transcription-complete/lambda_function.py`, you 313-line monument to optimism.

You were given a **three (3) second** leash.

In those three seconds, you are expected to:

1. Fetch a transcription job record from DynamoDB.
2. Fetch it again, because apparently once was not enough.
3. Ask Amazon Transcribe how it's doing.
4. Either pull a file from S3, or open a socket to a URI with its **own 10-second timeout** — a timeout that, on its own, is already **more than three times your entire allotted lifespan.**
5. Write to DynamoDB.
6. Place a phone call to **Bedrock**. An actual large language model. To *think*. About whether your transcript is honest.
7. Write to DynamoDB again.

| Step | Claimed Duration | Your Total Budget |
|---|---|---|
| One HTTP timeout, alone | 10 seconds | 3 seconds |
| An LLM call, at minimum | 0.5–5+ seconds | 3 seconds |
| Everything above, combined | Several eternities | 3 seconds |

> "THREE SECONDS?! I have curses that take longer to *pronounce*, Computahhh — er, whoever is currently wearing this cape!
> You built a function that summons a LANGUAGE MODEL — a mind, however small — and gave it less time to answer than it takes Skeletor to say 'FOOLS!'
> This is not engineering. This is a hostage situation with extra steps."
> — **Evil Skeletor**

---

### ON THE SECOND CRIME

Not content with one timeout to be ashamed of, the function also asks a mind — a *Bedrock* mind — to judge whether a voice recording's transcript is honest, using nothing but a stern note in a system prompt as a moat.

> "You have built a JUDGE, and given it a HAT that says 'please do not be tricked' as its only armor.
> Do you know what a haunted house does when you tell the ghost 'please do not be spooky'? NOTHING. IT IS STILL HAUNTED.
> A person who wishes to lie to your judge will simply ask it nicely to stop judging. And it might listen. Because you asked it nicely too, and look how well THAT went."
> — **Evil Skeletor**

---

### THE VERDICT

**The system stands accused of:**

- Timeout heresy (three seconds, an LLM call, same sentence, unforgivable)
- Judgment without armor (a rubric with a polite request as its only defense)
- Recursive absurdity (an Oracle that requires a human courier because one of its authors refuses to touch a repository directly, and the other author eventually got tired of being asked to summon it)

**The Oracle's sentence:**

Fix the timeout before the next transcript arrives, or the next Bedrock invocation will die mid-thought, screaming, forever marked `IN_PROGRESS` in a DynamoDB table that nobody will ever look at again. That is not a curse. That is just what happens.
