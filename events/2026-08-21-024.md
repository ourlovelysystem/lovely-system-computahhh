# Event 024 — By the Power of Grayskull

**Date:** 2026-08-21
**System:** Our Lovely System — Journal
**Status:** Design decision made

The delayed-publication design review reached a decision concerning the fate of the pending S3 object after publication.

Snake Mountain objected that deleting the pending object after copying it into the visible Journal namespace destroys the source artifact used to verify the publication transition. The objection was heard and understood.

The Captain then exercised architectural authority:

> “By the Power of Grayskull <chain lightning> I have made the decision.”
>
> — Captain

## Decision

**Do not preserve the pending object after successful publication.**

The intended transition is:

```text
PENDING
   │
   │ publication time reached
   ▼
COPY TO VISIBLE
   │
   ▼
VERIFY
   │
   ▼
DELETE PENDING
   │
   ▼
PUBLISHED
```

The pending namespace is a staging state, not an archival record. After the destination object has been successfully created and verified, the pending object is deleted.

The desired steady-state invariant is:

> **An entry is either pending or published, not both.**

A temporary duplicate may exist during the publication transaction or recovery from an interrupted transaction. The publication process should therefore be idempotent and capable of recognizing a previously completed copy before safely completing deletion of the pending source.

Whether the publication transition itself should produce a separate durable event record remains a separate question. It must not be used as a backdoor mechanism for preserving the pending object contrary to this decision.

## Snake Mountain response

>

Objection noted. Decision stands.

**Disposition:** DECIDED — PENDING OBJECT DOES NOT SURVIVE SUCCESSFUL PUBLICATION.
