# Event 045 — Markets Should Permit Relationships to Thicken

**Date:** 2026-08-22
**System:** Our Lovely System — Computahhh
**Status:** Design principle recorded

The Captain clarified an important constraint on the emerging market architecture.

The intent is **not** to unbundle durable relationships merely because individual acts can be exposed to a market. Nor should the market be designed to resist retainer relationships or make them artificially costly.

A market can instead be an environment in which durable relationships **emerge through observed performance**.

## Relationship formation as a market outcome

Repeated participation can generate evidence:

- an auditor repeatedly inspects difficult things well;
- a developer repeatedly accepts appropriate work and rejects inappropriate work;
- a participant delivers reliably;
- mistakes are surfaced and corrected;
- review is survived;
- judgment becomes observable over time.

Repeated market interaction may therefore produce a progression such as:

```text
market interaction
    -> observed competence
    -> accumulated trust
    -> repeated selection
    -> durable relationship
```

The resulting relationship might be a retainer, employment relationship, partnership, standing engagement, reserved capacity arrangement, or another form not yet named.

## Relationships may thicken

A participant relationship may naturally move through degrees of commitment:

> **I will consider this one thing.**

> **I keep choosing you.**

> **Call me when you have these problems.**

> **Reserve some of your capacity for me.**

> **Come work with us.**

The market should not impose needless transaction costs that force established participants to repeatedly rediscover, rebid, reauthorize, or renegotiate relationships whose value has already become observable.

Such friction would destroy information the market itself produced.

## Retainer is not capture

A durable relationship is not inherently evidence of market failure.

A useful distinction is:

**Retainer:**

> **We have repeatedly observed value here, so we want continuing access to this participant.**

**Capture:**

> **The continuing relationship prevents alternatives, problems, or competing participants from being visible or considered.**

These conditions are not equivalent.

A lawyer may become **our lawyer**. An auditor may become **our auditor**. A developer may become **our developer**. Those relationships can coexist with an accessible Problem Market and Solution Market around them.

## Design principle

> **Markets should permit durable relationships without granting durable relationships ownership of the market.**

The market should permit relationships to thicken.

It need not continuously destroy relationships in order to demonstrate that it remains a market.

Employment and retainers are therefore not necessarily legacy structures to dismantle. They may be legitimate **emergent market structures** produced by accumulated trust, demonstrated competence, repeated selection, and mutual willingness to increase commitment.

**Disposition:** DURABLE RELATIONSHIPS RECOGNIZED AS LEGITIMATE MARKET OUTCOMES. MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE SHOULD NOT ARTIFICIALLY RESIST OR TAX RELATIONSHIP THICKENING. CAPTURE REMAINS DISTINCT FROM RETENTION.
