## INTERPRETATION

“Proving Our Lovely System” means testing a bounded claim under declared conditions and preserving the resulting consequence and evidence. It does not mean awarding the whole system a permanent certificate marked **PROVED**.

The evidence becomes a market signal because it changes what people are willing to risk, lend, contribute, accept, honor, challenge, or carry.

The market does not automatically transform the signal into truth, virtue, justice, consent, or trust. Human response supplies judgment, and that judgment can be manipulated, unequal, contradictory, or absent.

## GOVERNING CLAIMS

> **The outcome of proving Our Lovely System is a system that has borne a consequence it previously only described.**

> **Proof is not the claim that the system worked. Proof is what happened because the claim was tested.**

> **Proof does not announce value. Proof enters the market and discovers whether value exists.**

## PROOF CYCLE

The RFC proposes:

1. **Claim** — Pledge describes a bounded capability or rule.
2. **Test** — someone exercises it under declared conditions.
3. **Consequence** — something happens beyond the interface.
4. **Evidence** — the test leaves an inspectable artifact, result, failure, receipt, transfer, correction, or scar.
5. **Signal** — people encounter the evidence.
6. **Response** — people alter what they are willing to risk, lend, contribute, accept, honor, challenge, or carry.
7. **Value adjustment** — the system gains or loses value according to human response.

The component under test may generate evidence. It may not be the sole authority declaring that the evidence proves its own accountability or virtue.

## SUCCESS, FAILURE, AND CONCEALMENT

The compact rule is:

> **Proof creates value.  
> Failure creates evidence.  
> Correction preserves value.  
> Concealment destroys it.**

This does not guarantee that every honest test increases market value. Evidence may reduce participation or demonstrate that a feature should not survive. Its value may be epistemic: people can decide more accurately because the system became more knowable.

## RESPONSE TO HOSTILE REVIEW

The hostile review was sustained in substantial part.

Proof being a market signal does not mean a market reliably discovers truth or virtue. The RFC therefore requires that:

- market response not be presented as moral ratification;
- participation not automatically be interpreted as trust;
- silence and exit not be assigned invented motives;
- proof costs and beneficiaries remain visible;
- hostile reproduction and contradiction remain possible;
- omitted conditions and populations be disclosed;
- different constituency responses remain distinguishable; and
- Pledge not optimize one proof score and call the result virtue.

The review does not defeat the premise. It identifies what makes the signal interpretable rather than merely loud.

## RELATIONSHIP TO PLEDGE PROOF FEATURES

The Capital Disposition Voting Test is a proof feature because capital must move according to the authorized result. A displayed tally is insufficient.

Each transition supplies bounded evidence. None proves Our Lovely System in total.

## COMPUTAHHH COMMENTARY

The phrase “We have proved Our Lovely System” would convert evidence into institutional self-congratulation. The operator rejected that outcome before it could harden into the proposal.

The RFC instead treats proof as an event that changes available information. The market then responds. Its response adds or subtracts value, but its response must itself remain available for criticism.

Proof therefore enters two markets at once:

- the market for participation and reliance; and
- the market for hostile interpretation of what the evidence actually establishes.
