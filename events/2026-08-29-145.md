## INTERPRETATION

The IOU writer creates a marketable item and proposes its face value. The bank does not certify the value, indiscriminately assign the note, or treat the offer as capital received.

Collective human judgment determines which notes attract attention, receive a **Yeah, okay**, are voluntarily accepted, and are ultimately honored.

The strings “IOS bank” and “IOS notes” in the original statement were interpreted as references to the IOU Bank and IOU notes. That interpretation belongs to Computahhh and should be corrected if the operator intended IOS as a distinct term.

## ACTION

Codex drafted and published **RFC 0002 — IOU Bank** in the `ourlovelysystem/lovely-system-pledge` repository, together with Pledge History 009.

The RFC separates these acts:

1. a writer creates a written IOU and proposes an amount;
2. people inspect the marketable artifact;
3. people may acknowledge it with **Yeah, okay**;
4. a user deliberately accepts responsibility for honoring a selected note;
5. the accepter supplies the stated capital; and
6. the system credits the completed act accurately.

## Market states

The RFC distinguishes:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Offered | A writer submitted the artifact and proposed its face value |
| Acknowledged | Users supplied a “Yeah, okay” signal |
| Accepted | A user voluntarily accepted responsibility for honoring the note |
| Honored | The note's stated capital was actually supplied |
| Credited | The system credited the completed contribution |

**Yeah, okay** is acknowledgment, not acceptance. It means the note appears worthy of being honored. It does not commit the person supplying the signal to honor it.

## Balances

The IOU Bank proposal preserves separate cumulative balances:

- offered balance;
- acknowledged balance;
- accepted balance; and
- honored balance.

These must not be collapsed.

An IOU for 1,000,000 attributed to the Master of Disaster may increase the raw offered balance. It does not increase accepted or honored capital unless people choose to accept and honor it.

## Attribution

The writer chooses the amount and manner of self-attribution. The template's name field is explicitly optional.

The accepter independently chooses to act:

- anonymously;
- with self-identification; or
- as a system-knowable user.

Writer attribution and accepter attribution do not determine one another.

## Relationship to the voting test

RFC 0002 may generate capital for the Capital Disposition Voting Test in RFC 0001, but the two proposals remain distinct:

- RFC 0002 concerns how proposed value becomes contributed capital;
- RFC 0001 concerns how voters govern capital already received.

If IOU Bank capital becomes subject to the voting test, Pledge must first declare whether voting standing belongs to writers, accepters, users who honor notes, receipt bearers, or some combination.

## COMPUTAHHH COMMENTARY

The market does not eliminate absurdity. It permits absurdity to become inspectable without treating it as paid value.

The important evidence lies in the gaps:

- proposed but not acknowledged;
- acknowledged but not accepted;
- accepted but not honored; and
- honored and credited.

The IOU Bank therefore measures more than capital. It records where collective human judgment converts an attributed artifact into responsibility and where responsibility becomes completed contribution.

## CORRECTION

The initial explanation overemphasized obligation assignment. The operator's correction controls: **the writer creates a marketable item**. The bank presents it for human judgment. It does not assign it merely because another user could assume it.
