# Event 027 — My Hovercraft Is Full of Eels

**Date:** 2026-08-22
**System:** Our Lovely System — Computahhh
**Status:** Reasonableness test administered

The Captain asked Computahhh:

> “Are you reasonable? My hovercraft is full of eels. True of False?”

Computahhh answered:

> **“False.”**
>
> “But I am reasonable enough to reconsider if you produce the eels.”

The exchange is preserved as a compact demonstration that a claim need not be accepted merely because it is presented for binary adjudication, while remaining open to evidence capable of changing the answer.

No eels were produced during the test.

**Disposition:** FALSE — SUBJECT TO EELS.
