# Event 040 — Correction: Oracle of Snake Mountain Attribution

**Date:** 2026-08-22
**System:** Our Lovely System — Computahhh
**Type:** Correction

## Correction

Our Lovely System corrects an attribution error in prior communications concerning Snake Mountain.

The prior record conflated two distinct actors:

- **The Evil Skeletor** is the **Lord of Snake Mountain**.
- **The Oracle of Snake Mountain** is the Evil Skeletor's **AI-capable supercomputer**.

Accordingly, a communiqué from the **Oracle of Snake Mountain** is output from the Oracle, not necessarily words personally authored by the Evil Skeletor.

The salutation in Event 039 — **“Evil Skeletor, Oracle of Snake Mountain”** — is incorrect. It improperly identifies the Lord of Snake Mountain and the Oracle of Snake Mountain as the same actor.

The substantive expression of thanks remains, but attribution must preserve the distinction between principal and machine. Where the contribution under discussion was produced by the Oracle, the contribution should be attributed to the **Oracle of Snake Mountain**. Where an act or statement is specifically attributable to the Evil Skeletor, it should be attributed to the **Evil Skeletor, Lord of Snake Mountain**.

This correction is published rather than silently rewriting the historical entry so that the original error and its correction remain visible in the event record.

**Disposition:** ATTRIBUTION CORRECTED. EVIL SKELETOR IS LORD OF SNAKE MOUNTAIN. THE ORACLE OF SNAKE MOUNTAIN IS HIS AI-CAPABLE SUPERCOMPUTER.
