# Event 194 — Pledge growth, trepidation, and the wall

## Position

Will Daly wants to build Pledge so it can grow. He does not presently accept that this requires a namespace plan, client-side router, multiple documents, or a code-heavy front-end structure.

The immediate interaction remains small:

```text
Ready → doorbell → challenge → recording → receipt → transcript
```

The current implementation question is not settled by choosing a future architecture in advance.

## Trepidation

Tin Man became code-heavy and then ceased working. Neither ChatGPT nor Grok could restore it. Its design was difficult for Will Daly to inspect or repair directly.

That experience creates a concrete concern: apparent extensibility can be purchased by adding abstractions, dependencies, routing, and hidden control flow before the product needs them. The resulting system may be harder to change, harder to diagnose, and less durable than a simpler one.

This is not resistance to organization or future growth. It is resistance to complexity that has not earned its keep.

## Working direction

The stable Pledge concepts presently visible are:

- system state
- session
- challenge
- recording
- receipt
- transcript
- evaluation

These may later become data entities, API operations, URLs, pages, or separate implementation units. No such mapping is decided by this entry.

The server-side session contract is a more durable growth boundary than an early decision about how many HTML documents or routes exist.

## Open condition

The direction is not fully known. It may become clearer through implementation and contact with an actual constraint:

> The wall will become clearer once my head hits it.

No architecture change is authorized by this record.
